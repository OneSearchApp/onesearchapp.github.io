Tessera Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Tessera is a puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: Tessera does not collect your data.

What Tessera collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not send any information about you or your device to the developer or to any third party.

What stays on your device

Your settings, statistics, challenge progress, saved game and chosen look are stored on your device only, using the standard storage Apple provides to apps. Deleting the app deletes this information. The developer cannot see it.

In-app purchase

Tessera offers one optional one-time purchase that unlocks customizations and additional puzzle packs. Apple processes the purchase. The app and its developer receive no payment details, such as card numbers. The app only learns, through Apple, whether this Apple ID has unlocked the purchase. Apple's own privacy policy governs how it handles purchase information.

Third parties

Tessera contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software.

Children

Because Tessera collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
