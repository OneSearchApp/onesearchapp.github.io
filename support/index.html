OneSearch Support

OneSearch is an independent iPhone and iPad app for searching your OneNote notes and the files attached to them. It is not affiliated with Microsoft.

Contact

Email onesearchapp@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and what you saw.

Common questions

How long does the first sync take?

Notes are searchable within minutes. Files and scans are read one at a time and can take hours or days for large notebooks, because OneNote limits how fast apps can read. Each file becomes searchable as soon as it is read. Charging and Wi-Fi help.

A note or file shows as failed.

Open the Sync tab, tap Troubleshooting, and follow the steps. You can retry an item or choose Ignore this note.

Does OneSearch change my notes?

No. Access is read-only.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone or iPad only. See the privacy policy.

How do I remove OneSearch's access?

Sign out in the app, and revoke access at account.live.com/consent/Manage (personal accounts) or your organization's My Apps page.
