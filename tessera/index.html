How it plays

Score by area

When four of your pieces are the corners of a square, you score its area. A bigger square is worth more.

Any angle

Squares can sit upright or tilted. A diamond of four pieces counts just as much as a neat box.

Combos

Finish several squares with one piece for a combo bonus.

Block and build

One piece can build your next square and block your opponent's at the same time.

What's inside

Play the computer or a friend

Four levels from Easy to Expert, or pass and play on one device. Boards from 5×5 to 8×8.

31 challenge puzzles

Goals, special rules and pieces already on the board. Earn up to three stars on each.

Learn as you play

Field Notes on tactics, geometry, word origins, gems and rocks, plus built-in rules, lessons and tips.

Hints and assists

Undo, hints, a ghost preview of each move, and a view of threats and chances.

Make it yours

Choose your board, pieces and music. Light or dark, with haptics and gentle original music.

Private by design

No ads, no account, no tracking. Your games and settings stay on your device.

Free, with one optional unlock

Tessera is free to play, including Classic games at every difficulty, two-player games, hints, undo and the Warm-Up puzzle pack. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks all customizations and the Tactics, Hazards, Master and Grandmaster puzzle packs. There is no subscription.

About

Tessera is an independent homage to the classic squares games of the 1990s, with its own name, art and code. “Tessera” is the Latin word for the small tiles of a mosaic. Made by Russell Silver.
