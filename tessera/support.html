Tessera Support

A strategy game of squares for iPhone and iPad

How do I play?

Take turns placing a piece on any empty point. When four of your pieces are the corners of a square, you score the square's area. Squares can be upright or tilted at any angle. Finish more than one square with a single piece for a combo bonus. When the board is full, the higher score wins. The Rules button in the app has lessons, tips and demos.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Customize or Challenges, tap any locked item to open the unlock screen, then tap Restore Purchases. You need to be signed in to the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

Something is locked that I paid for.

Restore Purchases (above) fixes this in almost every case. If it does not, check that you are signed in to the right Apple ID in Settings and that you are online, then contact us.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does Tessera collect my data?

No. Your progress and settings stay on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
