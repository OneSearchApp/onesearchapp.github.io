OneSearch Privacy Policy

Effective: November 6, 2026

OneSearch is an independent iPhone and iPad app that makes your Microsoft OneNote notes and attachments easier to search. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Microsoft. Microsoft and OneNote are trademarks of the Microsoft group of companies.

The short version

OneSearch does not collect your data. Everything it reads from OneNote is processed and stored on your iPhone or iPad. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.

What OneSearch accesses

When you sign in with your Microsoft account, you give OneSearch read-only permission to your OneNote notebooks (Notes.Read) and basic profile information (User.Read). OneSearch uses this to download your notebook structure, note text, and attachments so it can search them. It does not change or delete anything in OneNote.

Where your data goes

  • On your device: note text, attachments, text recognized from images and scans, and the search index are stored in OneSearch's private storage on your iPhone or iPad. Text recognition and related-item suggestions run on the device.
  • To Microsoft: OneSearch talks only to Microsoft services (sign-in and the Microsoft Graph OneNote service) to sign you in and read your notes. Microsoft's own privacy statement applies to that traffic.
  • To the developer or anyone else: nothing. OneSearch has no analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services, and no server of its own.

Sign-in credentials

OneSearch never sees your Microsoft password. Sign-in happens on Microsoft's own screen. The resulting access tokens are kept in the iOS Keychain on your device.

Your control

  • Choose which notebooks are synced, and turn file reading on or off, in the app.
  • Signing out removes your sign-in from the device.
  • Deleting OneSearch removes all of its stored data from your iPhone or iPad.
  • You can revoke OneSearch's access at any time at account.live.com/consent/Manage (personal accounts) or through your organization's My Apps page (work or school accounts).

Children

OneSearch is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
